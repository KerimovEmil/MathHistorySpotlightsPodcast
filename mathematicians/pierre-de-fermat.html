Pierre de Fermat

Fermat's Last Theorem[Wiki ↗]

No three positive integers fit this equation for \(n > 2\).

\[ a^n + b^n = c^n \]

Fermat's Little Theorem[Wiki ↗]

A fundamental property of prime numbers.

\[ a^{p-1} \equiv 1 \pmod{p} \]

Fermat's Principle[Wiki ↗]

Light travels between two points along the path of least time.

\[ \delta \int_{A}^{B} dt = 0 \]
