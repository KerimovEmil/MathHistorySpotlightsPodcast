Alan Mathison Turing

In this episode of Math History Spotlights, we explore the extraordinary life, groundbreaking mathematics, and profound legacy of Alan Mathison Turing (1912 – 1954).


From his revolutionary 1936 paper introducing Universal Turing Machines and the limits of decidability, to his wartime codebreaking breakthroughs at Bletchley Park, and his pioneering work on morphogenesis and machine intelligence, we trace the ideas that laid the foundations of modern computer science and artificial intelligence.
