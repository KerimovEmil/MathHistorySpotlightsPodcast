Famous Equations

1. Pascal's Identity

The recurrence relation defining Pascal's Triangle.

$$ \binom{n}{k} = \binom{n-1}{k-1} + \binom{n-1}{k} $$

2. Principle of Hydrostatics

Pascal's law regarding pressure in a fluid.

$$ \Delta P = \rho g \Delta h $$
