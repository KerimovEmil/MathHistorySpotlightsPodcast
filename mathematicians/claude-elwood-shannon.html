Claude Elwood Shannon

The life of Claude Elwood Shannon - The Father of the Bit.


Information Entropy

The measure of uncertainty or information content in a source.

\[ H(X) = - \sum_{i} P(x_i) \log_2 P(x_i) \]

Channel Capacity

The upper bound on the rate of information transmission.

\[ C = B \log_2\left(1 + \frac{S}{N}\right) \]
