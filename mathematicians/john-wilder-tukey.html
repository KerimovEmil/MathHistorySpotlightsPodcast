Cooley–Tukey FFT Algorithm

An extremely efficient algorithm to compute the discrete Fourier transform and its inverse.

\[ X_k = \sum_{n=0}^{N-1} x_{n} e^{-\frac{i 2\pi}{N} k n} \]
