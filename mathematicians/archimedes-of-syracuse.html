Archimedes of Syracuse

Archimedes was the greatest mathematician of his age. His contributions in geometry revolutionised the subject and his methods anticipated the integral calculus. He was a practical man who invented a wide variety of machines including pulleys and the Archimidean screw pumping device.


In this episode of Math History Spotlights, we explore the life, mathematical breakthroughs, and enduring legacy of Archimedes of Syracuse.


From foundational early discoveries to major theorem breakthroughs, personal struggles, and monumental contributions to science, we trace the ideas that shaped mathematical history.
