Marie-Sophie Germain

The life of Marie-Sophie Germain - The Woman Who Invented Math to Be Heard


Germain's Identity

A factorization identity.

\[ a^4 + 4b^4 = (a^2 + 2b^2 + 2ab)(a^2 + 2b^2 - 2ab) \]

Sophie Germain Primes

A prime \(p\) where \(2p+1\) is also prime, crucial in her work on Fermat's Last Theorem.

\[ p \in \mathbb{P}, \quad 2p+1 \in \mathbb{P} \]
