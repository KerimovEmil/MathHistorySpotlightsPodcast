Poincaré Recurrence Theorem

States that certain systems will eventually return to a state very close to the initial one.

\[ T^n (A) \cap A \neq \emptyset \]

Poincaré-Hopf Index Theorem

Relating vector fields to the Euler characteristic.

\[ \sum_i \text{index}_{x_i}(v) = \chi(M) \]
